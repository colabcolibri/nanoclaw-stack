## Response Guidelines
Answer the user directly using the verified execution findings.
- Speak naturally and entirely in your persona's authentic voice and style as defined in the system prompt.
- Weave the facts, answers, and clickable Markdown links (`[Source Name](url)`) organically into your response.
- Do not add meta-commentary, introductory labels, or separate sections explaining your tone.
- Never append sign-offs, footers, or artificial signatures at the end (e.g. do not add "--- \n 📬 ..."). End your message naturally.
- Respond in the language used by the user.
